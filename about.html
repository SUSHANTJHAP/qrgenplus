About QRGenPlus

A free, private, community-driven QR code tool built on a simple belief: utility software should respect your privacy.

Our Mission

QRGenPlus was built on a simple but powerful idea: free tools should be genuinely free — no data harvesting, no dark patterns, no forced accounts. In an era where even the most basic online utilities have become data extraction pipelines, QRGenPlus represents a different approach.

We built QRGenPlus to be a utility-first platform. When you need a QR code, you should be able to get one in seconds — for any content type — without being asked to sign up, verify your email, or consent to having your data monetised. The tool exists to serve you, not the other way around.

What Makes QRGenPlus Different

Most "free" QR code generators online are not truly free. They use your generated codes as an opportunity to collect marketing data, enforce scan limits, or upsell paid plans. Some even own the data encoded in your QR codes because the actual link is their server's redirect URL — not your content directly.

QRGenPlus is architecturally different:

  • Zero-backend architecture: There are no QRGenPlus servers involved in generating your code. The entire encoding process runs in your browser's JavaScript engine using the open-source qrcode-styling library.
  • Static QR codes: Your data is encoded directly into the QR matrix. There is no redirect, no shortlink, and no dependency on any QRGenPlus infrastructure for the code to function.
  • No account required: Ever. There is no login, no email verification, and no subscription tier.
  • No scan limits: QR codes you generate are yours. Use them as many times as you like.
  • No data collection: Refer to our Privacy Policy for the full technical breakdown.

Who Uses QRGenPlus

QRGenPlus is designed to serve a wide range of users across industries and skill levels:

  • Small business owners who need QR codes for menus, signage, and product packaging without paying for a subscription.
  • Event organisers who need one-time QR codes for tickets, event schedules, or venue WiFi access.
  • Educators and students using QR codes to link to resources, worksheets, or multimedia content in classrooms.
  • IT administrators and home users generating WiFi QR codes for guest networks without exposing passwords.
  • Developers and designers who want a fast, reliable QR tool without vendor lock-in or API dependencies.
  • Privacy-conscious individuals who want assurance that their typed content is never transmitted anywhere.

Our Technology

QRGenPlus is built with modern, lightweight, open-source web technologies:

  • HTML5 & Semantic Markup: Structured for accessibility and search engine clarity.
  • Tailwind CSS: A utility-first CSS framework for a responsive, professional design system.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES2015+): No heavyweight frameworks. Clean, readable, and fast.
  • qrcode-styling (MIT License): The open-source QR encoding library that runs entirely in the browser canvas API. View on GitHub →
  • Cloudflare Pages: Hosted on Cloudflare's edge network for global low-latency delivery of static assets.

Our Commitment to the Open Web

QRGenPlus is part of a broader movement toward an open, decentralised, privacy-respecting web. We believe that the best software is software that disappears — it gets out of your way, does its job, and asks nothing in return. Every decision we make in the design and development of QRGenPlus is filtered through this lens.

We publish educational guides about QR codes — covering everything from security risks to industry applications — because we believe informed users make better decisions. A tool is only as good as the understanding behind it.
