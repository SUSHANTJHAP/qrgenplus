Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about QRGenPlus and QR code generation.

Is QRGenPlus completely free to use? +

Yes. QRGenPlus is 100% free with no subscriptions, no paywalls, and no account required. Simply open the tool, enter your content, and download your QR code as a PNG file instantly.

Does QRGenPlus store or collect my data? +

No. QRGenPlus never stores, transmits, or logs any data you enter. All QR code generation — including WiFi credentials, URLs, and text — happens exclusively inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

What types of QR codes can I generate? +

QRGenPlus supports four QR code types: (1) URL/Link QR codes for any website, (2) Text QR codes for plain messages, phone numbers, or emails, (3) WiFi QR codes supporting WPA, WPA2, WEP, and open networks, and (4) File QR codes via Google Drive, Dropbox, or Imgur links.

Can I use the generated QR codes commercially? +

Yes. All QR codes generated by QRGenPlus are yours to use freely, including for commercial purposes such as business cards, product packaging, restaurant menus, and event materials. There are no licensing restrictions on the output.

How does the WiFi QR code work? +

The WiFi QR code uses the standard WIFI:T:WPA;S:NetworkName;P:Password;; protocol. When scanned by a smartphone camera (iOS 11+ / Android 10+), the phone will automatically offer to join the network — no app required. Your password is only encoded in the local QR image and never transmitted.

Do the generated QR codes expire? +

QRGenPlus generates static QR codes that never expire — they work forever as long as the content they point to remains accessible. Unlike dynamic QR codes, the data is fixed in the pattern at generation time and cannot be edited afterward.

What format does the downloaded QR code use? +

QR codes are downloaded as high-resolution PNG files directly via your browser. PNG is ideal for both digital use and professional print materials. The file is named qrgenplus-code.png.
