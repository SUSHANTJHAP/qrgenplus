About QRGenPlus

A QR code platform built to be genuinely useful — free static codes for everyone, and powerful dynamic codes for businesses that need analytics and flexibility.

Our Mission

QRGenPlus was built on a simple belief: great tools should be accessible. We offer free, unlimited static QR codes for anyone who needs them — no login required, no scan limits, no watermarks. For businesses and professionals who need more — real-time scan analytics, editable destinations, custom short URLs, and PDF uploads — we offer a Pro subscription at a straightforward price.

We built QRGenPlus because most QR code tools on the market are either too limited, too expensive, or too loaded with dark patterns. We wanted to change that.

What QRGenPlus Offers

QRGenPlus is a two-tier platform:

  • Free static QR codes — Generate QR codes for URLs, Wi-Fi, vCards, SMS, email, and plain text. All encoding happens in your browser. No account required. Download as PNG, SVG, or WEBP.
  • Pro dynamic QR codes — Create short-link QR codes whose destination URL can be changed at any time without reprinting the code. Includes real-time scan analytics (country, device, OS, browser), password protection, custom short IDs, and smart device routing (iOS vs Android).

What Makes Us Different

Most "free" QR code generators online are not truly free. They use your codes as an opportunity to collect marketing data, enforce scan limits, or show intrusive upsells. QRGenPlus approaches this differently:

  • Static QR codes are truly free: Your data is encoded directly into the QR image. There is no redirect, no shortlink, and no dependency on any QRGenPlus server for the code to work.
  • No scan limits on static codes: QR codes you generate are yours forever. Use them as many times as you like.
  • Transparent pricing for Pro: Dynamic QR codes are a Pro feature with a clear, simple subscription. No hidden fees or per-scan charges.
  • Privacy-first analytics: Scan tracking collects country-level geolocation, device type, OS, and browser. We do not track individual users or collect personal data from scanners.

Who Uses QRGenPlus

QRGenPlus is used by a wide range of people and organisations:

  • Small business owners who need QR codes for menus, signage, and product packaging.
  • Event organisers who use dynamic QR codes for e-tickets, venue information, and registration forms.
  • Educators and students linking worksheets and classroom displays to digital resources.
  • Marketing teams tracking scan performance across print and OOH campaigns using Pro analytics.
  • IT administrators generating Wi-Fi QR codes for guest networks.
  • Developers and designers who want a fast, reliable QR tool without API lock-in.

Our Technology

QRGenPlus is built with modern web technologies:

  • Frontend: HTML5, vanilla JavaScript, and custom CSS for a fast, accessible experience across all devices.
  • QR Generation: Client-side QR encoding using the open-source qrcode-styling library (MIT License) — static codes never leave your browser.
  • Backend (Pro features): Node.js with Express 5, PostgreSQL database, and Razorpay for subscription management.
  • Infrastructure: Hosted on a secure cloud server with Cloudflare as our CDN and DDoS protection layer.
