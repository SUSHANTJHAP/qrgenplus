Why People Search for Canva QR Code Generator

Canva is a popular graphic design platform that includes a QR code generator within its suite of tools. It's a reasonable choice if you're already using Canva to design a flyer or business card and want to embed a QR code directly into your design. However, for pure QR code generation — without any design work — Canva is overkill.

Canva requires a free account at minimum, and its most flexible QR code features (like advanced customisation and bulk generation) are restricted to Canva Pro subscribers. More importantly, Canva is a design platform first and a QR tool second. Its QR generator is intentionally embedded within the design workflow — you can't just visit a URL, paste a link, and download a QR code in under 10 seconds without navigating through their design editor.
