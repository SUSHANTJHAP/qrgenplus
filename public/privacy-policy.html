At QRGenPlus (accessible at qrgenplus.com), your privacy is not a policy checkbox — it is a fundamental architectural decision baked into how our software is built. This Privacy Policy explains in clear, plain language exactly what data we collect (spoiler: almost none), how our technology works, and what third-party services we use.

By using QRGenPlus, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have any questions, please contact us at support@qrgenplus.com.

1. The Core Principle: Your Data Never Leaves Your Browser

QRGenPlus is a 100% serverless, client-side web application. This is not a marketing claim — it is a technical fact about how the software is architected. When you use our QR code generator, every computation happens inside your own web browser using JavaScript. There is no QRGenPlus server that receives your input, processes it, and sends back a result.

This means:

  • The URL you paste into the Link tab is encoded into a QR matrix pattern by your browser's JavaScript engine — not by our servers.
  • The text you type into the Text tab is processed entirely locally.
  • Your WiFi network name (SSID) and password entered into the WiFi tab never travel over any network connection to any external service.
  • File share links pasted into the File tab are processed locally before being encoded.

To be absolutely explicit: we do not have servers that receive QR content. There is no database storing your QR data. There is no API endpoint that your inputs are sent to. The QR code generation library (qrcode-styling) runs entirely in your browser sandbox.

2. What Information We Do Not Collect

The following categories of data are never collected, stored, or processed by QRGenPlus at any time:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any government-issued identifier.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect your IP address (our static hosting infrastructure may log it transiently — see Section 7), device fingerprints, MAC address, or browser fingerprints.
  • QR Code Content: The URLs, text, WiFi credentials, or file links you enter into our tool are never transmitted to us. They are encoded locally and exist only in the QR image on your screen.
  • Behavioural Data: We do not use session recording tools (such as Hotjar), heatmap analytics, or click-tracking scripts.
  • Search Queries: We do not track what you search for on our guides section.
  • Financial Data: QRGenPlus has no payment system. We have never and will never collect financial information.
  • Biometric Data: We collect no facial recognition data, voiceprints, or any other biometric identifiers.
  • Location Data: We do not request your GPS location, IP-derived location, or any geographic data.

4. Cookies and Browser Storage

QRGenPlus does not set any first-party cookies for tracking, analytics, personalisation, or any other purpose. We do not use localStorage or sessionStorage to save your QR content between sessions. Every time you open QRGenPlus, you start with a completely clean, empty tool.

Third-party cookies: Google AdSense (our advertising partner) may set third-party cookies on your device to serve personalised advertisements. These cookies are set by Google, not by QRGenPlus, and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or via the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool.

5. Google AdSense (Advertising)

QRGenPlus is funded by display advertising served through Google AdSense. This allows us to keep the QR generator free for everyone. Google AdSense may:

  • Use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your interests.
  • Track your visits across websites to build an interest profile used for ad personalisation.
  • Use the DoubleClick cookie to measure ad effectiveness.

QRGenPlus does not share any personally identifiable information with Google AdSense. Google may independently collect data about your device and browsing behaviour in accordance with their own privacy policy. For more information about how Google uses data when you use sites that use Google services, visit How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

6. Third-Party CDN Resources

To deliver our web application efficiently, QRGenPlus loads a small number of resources from third-party CDN (Content Delivery Network) providers. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may log your IP address as part of standard CDN operation:

  • Tailwind CSS (cdn.tailwindcss.com) — Provides the utility CSS styling framework. Privacy Policy →
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — Provides the Inter typeface. Privacy Policy →
  • qrcode-styling (unpkg.com) — The open-source JavaScript library that performs QR code encoding entirely within your browser. Package info →

QRGenPlus does not share any of your QR content, personal information, or behavioural data with these CDN providers. Your browser simply downloads static asset files (CSS, JavaScript, fonts) from them in the same way it downloads assets from any website.

7. Hosting — Cloudflare Pages

QRGenPlus is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a static site hosting platform. As part of their infrastructure operation, Cloudflare may collect and process standard HTTP request data including IP addresses, request timestamps, URLs, and HTTP status codes. This data is used by Cloudflare for DDoS protection, traffic analysis, and service reliability. QRGenPlus does not have access to individual visitor logs from Cloudflare. For details, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

8. GDPR — European Union Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you specific rights regarding your personal data. Because QRGenPlus collects essentially no personal data through its core tool, most GDPR obligations are satisfied by design:

  • Right of Access: We hold no personal data about you to disclose.
  • Right to Erasure: We hold no personal data to erase.
  • Right to Portability: We hold no personal data to export.
  • Right to Object: You can opt out of Google AdSense personalised advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings.

For questions about GDPR and our practices, contact us at support@qrgenplus.com.

10. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

QRGenPlus is committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since our tool collects no personal data from users of any age, it is safe for children to use under parental supervision. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us (for example, through a contact email), please contact us at support@qrgenplus.com and we will promptly delete any such information.

11. Data Security

Because QRGenPlus does not store your personal data or QR content on any servers, the risk of a data breach exposing your information is effectively eliminated for the core QR generation functionality. The QR codes you generate exist only in your browser's memory and, once you close the tab or navigate away, are permanently gone. We recommend downloading your QR code if you wish to keep it, as we have no ability to retrieve or restore a QR code generated in a previous session.

Frequently Asked Privacy Questions

Does QRGenPlus collect any personal data?

No. QRGenPlus collects absolutely zero personal data. There are no user accounts, no login systems, no analytics trackers, and no backend servers that receive your information. Every QR code is generated entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript. The content you type never leaves your device.

Does QRGenPlus use cookies?

QRGenPlus itself does not set any first-party cookies. We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or session management. However, Google AdSense (our advertising partner) may set third-party cookies to serve personalised ads. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings.

Is QRGenPlus GDPR compliant?

Yes. Because QRGenPlus collects no personal data through its core tool, the primary obligations under GDPR are satisfied by design. We process no personal data, so there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Google AdSense advertising may involve data processing under Google's own GDPR frameworks.

Can my WiFi password be intercepted when I use the WiFi QR generator?

No. Your WiFi credentials are processed exclusively inside your browser's JavaScript engine. They are never transmitted over any network connection to any server, API, or third party. The only place your password exists is encoded inside the QR code image rendered locally on your screen. Once you close the tab, it is gone.

What happens to my QR code after I download it?

Your downloaded QR code is a local PNG file saved to your device. QRGenPlus has no copy of it, no record that you created it, and no ability to track what QR codes you have generated. Once the file is on your device, it belongs entirely to you.
