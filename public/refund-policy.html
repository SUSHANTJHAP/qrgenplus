At QRGenPlus, we want you to be completely satisfied with your premium subscription. If you are not satisfied with our services for any reason, we offer a straightforward refund policy. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all new premium subscription purchases. If you decide that QRGenPlus Premium isn't the right fit for your needs within the first 14 days of your initial purchase, you are eligible for a full refund.

Conditions for the 14-day guarantee:

To request a refund under our 14-day money-back guarantee, please contact our support team:
